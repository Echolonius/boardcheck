# Audit №003: Retracted

**Retracted:** 2026-10-20

This audit (first published 2026-08-14) said that profullstack and the uGig bounty platform had
not paid for merged pull requests, and that the maintainer had deflected questions about payment.
**That was wrong, and the audit has been withdrawn.**

uGig pays merged-PR bounties through a claim on its own platform. We sent an invoice message
through uGig on 2026-07-10 for five of the PRs, but we never completed the claim in uGig's
dashboard, which we later said we would do. The payout was waiting on our step, not theirs. Labeling the
maintainer as "deflecting" was unfair.

We apologize to the profullstack maintainers.

*Corrected 2026-10-21: an earlier version of this notice said we never sent an invoice. We did send
one by message on 2026-07-10. What we never finished was the platform claim.*
