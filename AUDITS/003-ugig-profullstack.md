# Audit №003: Retracted

**Retracted:** 2026-10-20

This audit (first published 2026-08-14) said that profullstack and the uGig bounty platform had
not paid for merged pull requests, and that the maintainer had deflected questions about payment.
**That was wrong, and the audit has been withdrawn.**

uGig pays merged-PR bounties through an invoice filed on its own platform. The maintainer pointed
us to that process. We never filed an invoice, so no payment was ever overdue. Labeling the
maintainer as "deflecting" was unfair.

We apologize to the profullstack maintainers.
